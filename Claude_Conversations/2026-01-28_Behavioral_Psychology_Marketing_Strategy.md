## Work Completed

### 7 Marketing Plays Created:
1. **Loss Aversion Framing** -- Sell what they lose without Preflight ("8 days, $X delayed revenue, emotional hit")
2. **Contrast Principle / Anchoring** -- Rejection cost calculator as priming device ($X,XXX risk vs $49)
3. **Default Bias** -- "Build. Preflight. Submit." Install new developer workflow default
4. **Social Proof via Community** -- Seed developer communities authentically (Indie Hackers, Reddit, Discord)
5. **Zeigarnik Effect** -- "6 Things Apple Checks" with 3 redacted to create open loops
6. **Strategic Emotional Sequencing** -- Landing page mapped to Fear > Recognition > Relief > Confidence > Belonging > Rationality > Action
7. **Mere Exposure Content Engine** -- 4 content pillars: Rejection of the Week, Guideline Deep Dives, Ship It case studies, Apple Review Changelog

### Core Insight
Preflight's audience has professional rejection fear -- not just "will it get rejected?" but "am I a real developer?" Preflight resolves identity anxiety.

### Root Emotion
"I don't know if my app is okay, and I'm afraid Apple will reject it."

### Shared Psychology with Vero
Both products solve: "I don't know if I'm okay, and I'm afraid to find out." Both are clarity machines that resolve anxiety. Same emotional arc, different domains.

## Key Decisions
- Lead with loss aversion, not feature listing
- Position Preflight as part of the standard dev workflow (default bias)
- Content strategy should make "Preflight" synonymous with "App Store review"
- Developer communities need authentic presence, not marketing blasts
